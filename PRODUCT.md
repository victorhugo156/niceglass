# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences share one page. A buyer opens it to choose a suncatcher. The same page has to read as the maker's studio, not only as a catalog.

## Product Purpose

Nice Glass shows handmade stained-glass suncatchers and the way one maker produces them, so a buyer can choose a piece. Success today is a buyer who understands a specific piece well enough to ask about it. Success later includes taking an order or a payment through the product. That later path is a direction, not a built capability.

## Positioning

One maker, working in small runs, cuts stained glass, wraps it in copper foil, and joins it with a visible solder line. Pieces are sold one at a time. The public store that exists now is the Nice Glass Etsy shop. A catalog that hid the maker, or a cart that treated pieces as identical stock, would not be this product.

## Operating Context

The current surface is one web page: home, the pieces, the studio, how a piece is made, and contact. A buyer evaluating a piece is promised daylight photographs, the exact size, and how it hangs. The maker's process, as the page already states it, is sketch, cut, foil, solder, finish.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed:

- Customer-facing name is **Nice Glass**. Always use that capitalization.
- Voice stays first person: one maker, small runs, copper foil, visible solder.
- Current pieces are Frog, Sushi, Moon, and Meltem.
- Pieces are sold one at a time.
- Prices are displayed in Australian dollars (AUD).
- Instagram stays as a channel. Its URL is undecided.
- The Etsy shop is https://www.etsy.com/au/shop/NiceGlassSuncatchers

Undecided, and not to be invented:

- The AUD amount for each piece. The euro figures currently on the page are not an approved price list.
- The Instagram URL.
- Whether WhatsApp remains a contact channel. It is on the page today and was not confirmed.
- How on-site ordering or payment will work when that capability is built.
- Whether the unused piece files `bora`, `lodos`, and `sarma` ever become catalog. They are not current pieces.

## Brand Commitments

The customer-facing name is Nice Glass. The maker speaks in the first person. Instagram and the Etsy shop above are the confirmed public channels.

## Evidence on Hand

- Page copy and piece list: `src/pages/index.tsx`
- Piece images: `public/images/pieces/` (frog, sushi, moon, meltem, plus bora, lodos, and sarma, which are not catalog)
- Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/au/shop/NiceGlassSuncatchers

No testimonials, press, case studies, or approved AUD price list exist in the project. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The page serves the buyer who is choosing and the maker whose studio it is.
- A piece is individual: one at a time, with daylight photographs, size, and how it hangs before a buyer commits.
- The catalog is the four current pieces. Files that are not on the page are not available work.
- Customer-facing writing uses the name Nice Glass and a first-person maker voice.
- Money is in AUD. Commerce that exists today is the Etsy shop; on-site ordering is a later capability with an undecided mechanism.
